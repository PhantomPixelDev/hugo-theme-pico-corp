For six years our site said "contact us for pricing". The result was predictable:
roughly half of first calls ended with both sides discovering the budget was off by
a factor of four, after forty minutes nobody got back.

Now the site says a project typically runs between €45,000 and €160,000. The
volume of enquiries fell by about a quarter. The proportion that became proposals
roughly doubled.

We shortlisted three studios in an afternoon because two of them published
ranges. The one that would not say anything never made the list.

## What a range communicates

It answers the only question a prospective client has in the first thirty seconds:
are these people plausible for us? Withholding that answer does not protect
negotiating position. It just moves the disqualification to a later, more expensive
point in the process.

It also sets an expectation about what kind of studio we are. Nine senior people
in Berlin are not competing on price with a fifteen-person offshore team, and
pretending otherwise wastes everyone's afternoon.

## Why the range is wide

Because scope decides cost, and we do not know the scope yet. The honest version
is: two weeks of discovery, then a fixed price. Any studio quoting a precise figure
before understanding the problem is either guessing or planning to make it up in
change requests.

## What we lost

Some enquiries from organisations with smaller budgets who might have been talked
into a smaller scope. That is a genuine cost, and we accept it. The alternative
was a calendar full of calls that ended politely and produced nothing.
