Plenty of organisations do not need a studio. They need two days of senior
attention to settle an argument, confirm a hunch, or tell a board that the
platform is fine and the problem is elsewhere.

We keep a small amount of capacity for that kind of work. It is deliberately
separated from delivery, and we will say when the honest recommendation is to
change nothing.
