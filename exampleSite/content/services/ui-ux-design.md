Consumer design advice rarely survives contact with a professional tool. A
dispatcher looking at four hundred rows does not want generous whitespace; they
want density, keyboard shortcuts and a table that never reflows.

So we design for the job. That usually means fewer decorative decisions, tighter
type scales, and a great deal of attention to states: loading, empty, error,
partially permitted, and the one nobody documented.
