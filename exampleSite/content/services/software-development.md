The tools that run a mid-sized business are usually a decade of accumulated
workarounds: a spreadsheet that became load-bearing, an Access database nobody
will touch, an integration that runs on one person's laptop.

Replacing that is less about technology than about understanding the exceptions.
We spend the first two weeks with the people who handle those exceptions, because
they are where the real business rules live.
