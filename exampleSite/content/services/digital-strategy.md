Strategy work goes wrong in two ways: it stays so abstract that nobody can act
on it, or it becomes a feature list with no argument behind it.

We keep it short on purpose. Four weeks is enough to gather evidence, size the
options and write down a recommendation. Longer than that and the organisation
has usually changed underneath the analysis.
